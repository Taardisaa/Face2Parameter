## 2 Objective (scoring domain)

See the `objective.py` docstring. In short:

- **Front (yaw 0) and three-quarter (yaw +30): the user-approved step-2 master.** These are the
  raw MediaPipe jaw chains on the art:
  - front: both sides, 21 points;
  - three-quarter: near side, 11 points.

  They are compared with the same model, options (thresholds 0.1) and point ids on the candidate's
  rendered bridge target, using a fixed detector rectangle (the full frame).
- **Profiles (yaw ±90): face silhouette against the plain background**, measured on both the art
  and the render.
  - Why not MediaPipe: measured on the baseline renders with ±1-level image noise (8 seeds), the
    profile chain moves 13–16 px on average (up to 32 px), and the left profile loses the face in
    5 of 8 seeds. Front and three-quarter move 0.5–0.7 px (max 1.1–1.5 px). Live and offline
    profile renders that differ by 1 level give chain differences of 9 px (right) and 17 px (left).
    The profile MediaPipe output is therefore not a measurement.
  - Stations: fixed art stations every 6 art px of arclength. They run from the lower lip (art
    landmark 17) down the chin front, past the chin (152), and along the under-chin to the throat
    junction (first contour tangent within 35° of straight down). The right profile has 52
    stations and the left 41. This is todo item 7's path from the lower lip to the under-chin.
  - Residual: the signed distance along each station normal to the render silhouette mapped into
    art pixels. Positive means the render lies outside the art outline.
  - Silhouette contract: the iso-line at 12 RGB units from the per-row background median of a
    24-px border strip on the face side (marching squares).
  - Not measured in profile: the internal jaw-angle line. It is covered by the front and
    three-quarter chains.
- **Comparison frame, frozen on the baseline.**
  - A 2-D similarity maps the step-4 mesh-bound upper-face anchors (`*_anchor_bindings.json`:
    eye corners, nose, forehead/temple surface points) to the art detections of the same ids.
  - The anchors are projected through the renderer's own skin stream (slot 0, frame N−2) and
    bridge camera (render 0, non-jittered).
  - Groups are weighted equally.
  - Profiles keep the anchor rotation and scale and are translated to the fixed lower lip
    (todo item 7). The front MediaPipe 17 is ray-cast onto the head surface, projected into the
    profile and snapped to the render silhouette (1.5 / 3.5 px), then placed on the art's
    lower-lip silhouette point.
- **Residual units:** art px / view eye unit / √n, so each view contributes its RMS in eye units
  (the `predictor_objective.py` weighting).

Baseline (DINOv2 v1 card, hair-off domain, `45ac5b1` templates; live PNG and offline baseline score identically): cost 0.008522. Per-view RMS:

## 3 Parameters, bounds and guard

`controls.py` uses the handoff's 36 controls (`guarded_predictor_fit.py`):

- **Groups:** cf_J_ChinTip_s, cf_J_ChinLow, cf_J_Chin_rs and the mirrored pair cf_J_CheekLow_L/R.
- **Channels and ranges:**
  - scale × (1 ± 0.10);
  - position ± 0.025 for the central bones, ± 0.012 for the pair;
  - rotation ± 3°.
- **Axes:** x, y, z, with q ∈ [−1, 1].

Why these bones: they are the bones whose skinned region is the chin, jaw body and lower cheek.
None is an ancestor of the mouth, eye or nose bones, and none is a head-surface root. The
renderer's `dependency_check` confirms this per candidate. native59 stays fixed.

**Persistent ABMX encoding.** All five bones are re-expressed with length exactly 1 and
PositionModifier = card-load local − `_posBaseline`, taken from the forward model's card-load
`CollectBaseline`. Installed HS2ABMX re-normalises the current position for non-unit lengths,
which drifts once a position offset is added. The encoded q = 0 reproduces the card's skin streams
bit for bit (0 changed transforms, same target SHA).

**Feature guard (`protection.py`).** This is the `feature_protection.py` rule, evaluated on the
renderer's own slot-0 streams through each window's bridge camera:

- o_head protected vertices ≤ 2 px;
- o_namida protected vertices ≤ 1 px (its jaw part follows the native skinning);
- every other surface ≤ 1 px;
- minimum 3-D oriented triangle-normal ratio ≥ 0.2.

Protected means feature weight > 0.05, cf_J_Head-local y > 0.25, inside the front lip box ± 4 px,
or no jaw weight. o_head has 3865 protected and 574 jaw-following vertices; o_namida has 714 and
124. Infeasible proposals are not rendered and get the baseline residual plus 20× the violation,
as in the earlier guarded fit.
